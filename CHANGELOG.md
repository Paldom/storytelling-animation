### Added
- `storytelling-animation`: end-to-end orchestrator over the six pipeline skills with a gate per step and a disk-based pipeline status script.
- `animation-storyboard`: brief → validated `storyboard.json` (contract v1) with reading-hold timing, transitions, budgets and loop semantics.
- `vector-asset-sourcing`: icon/illustration/Lottie pack selection with a license matrix, an assets manifest checker and attribution output.
- `vector-scene-layout`: Remotion scaffold, per-target design tokens (safe areas, type scale, stroke, contrast), templates and per-beat still renders.
- `smooth-motion-choreography`: motion vocabulary with easing/spring tokens, loop-wrap Video template, animated card, and a flicker/strobe linter.
- `animation-export-presets`: sourced LinkedIn/PowerPoint/GIF/custom presets, master-plus-derivative renderer with a license gate, and an ffprobe/atom-order/decode checker.
- `animation-frame-qa`: bounded contact sheet, phone-width stills, safe-area overlay, loop seam strip, luminance flash report and a look-at-it checklist.
- Repository scaffolded from the skills template.
